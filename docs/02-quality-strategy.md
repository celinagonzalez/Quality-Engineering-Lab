## Definition of Done — Discovery / Enabler Stories

This Definition of Done applies to all **Enabler Stories** related to discovery
and business analysis activities across the Quality Engineering Lab. It is
defined at the lab level (not per story) to keep consistency across case
studies and team roles.

A Discovery / Enabler Story is considered **Done** when:

- [ ] Discovery Report completed
- [ ] Business Glossary published
- [ ] Risk Analysis reviewed
- [ ] Markdown validated
- [ ] Files committed to GitHub
- [ ] Linked to Jira Story
